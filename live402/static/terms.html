Terms

What a check is.

402Signal checks the endpoint, the price and the recipient before your agent pays, on x402 and MPP, and returns a signed record of what it saw. The agent remains responsible for the payment it then makes.

Operator and governing law

402Signal is a trading name. The service is operated by an individual based in Virginia, United States; the contact for every question about these terms is ross@402signal.com. These terms and any dispute about the service are governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia, United States, without regard to its conflict-of-law rules. If the service moves to a company, this section will name it.

Billing

A successful hosted check is billed at the published price on the pricing page. Typed misses are not billed. Platform plans are invoiced by email in USDC; there is no automatic renewal.

Limits

A check is not settlement, custody, or a guarantee that the seller will deliver. A catalog listing is not an endorsement. The public log and tests are not an independent audit.

You must not use the service to probe private networks, abuse third-party endpoints, or submit payment material you do not control.

Availability

The service may be unavailable. Anchoring and rail status can lag. Keep the original authorization and budget when a call is uncertain; do not create a second authorization just to retry.
