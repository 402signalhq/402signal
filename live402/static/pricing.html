Platforms and wallet operators

Evaluate one real payment workflow.

For teams that already operate an agent, wallet or paid API client. Start with a supported integration and a written result to reproduce. Platform work is scoped individually; there is no self-serve subscription or hosted evidence archive included by default.

Define the evaluation

Choose one workload, exact payment profile, recurring seller endpoints and an integration owner. Agree a time limit, credit allowance, capacity and any paid support before starting. Trial credits are operator-issued, not unlimited.

Demonstrate the boundary

A matching synthetic offer reaches your test callback once. Changed price, recipient and expired evidence stop it. Verify a saved record from your own storage, and rehearse uncertain-outcome recovery before a funded run.

Decide what to keep

Review added latency, compatibility failures, support effort and the usefulness of the retained evidence. Agree the ongoing deliverables and price before any paid pilot or recurring service begins. A logo or reference is optional and separately approved.

Storage, alerts and billing

You store the private evidence. The current product returns the original request's evidence, receipt and private reveal to the buyer. Your team keeps the exact request and raw response, approved policy and wallet records in storage it controls. The evaluation can scope help with this workflow; a general hosted retention or export service is not included.

Private response recovery lasts 120 seconds after the request began. It is not a retention window or backup. Your storage policy determines access, export, deletion and retention after an evaluation ends. Any additional hosted storage would require a separate written scope and data terms.

Signed alerts require an admission key and fire when a check observes a change. They do not include continuous or scheduled monitoring. Agree who performs any scheduled observations, their cadence and cost.

A written platform quote must state included work, credits or usage, overages, support and the billable event. Permissionless checks continue to use the published tariff unless the agreement explicitly supplies credits or different billing. An admission key alone does not waive checking fees. Invoicing is manual; no automatic subscription starts from an enquiry.

Existing agreed Design partner terms, including a free quarter where already accepted, are honored. New evaluations use an agreed scope and timetable.

What the fee is and is not

One fee, clearly bounded.

  • Seller charges, deposits, network fees and rent are separate from the checking fee and are paid by your wallet to the seller directly.
  • A later buyer refusal does not reverse an already-settled check. A qualifying offer is not a guarantee of delivery or output quality.
  • If a settlement ends in an unknown state, never reuse that authorization; use the read-only recovery contract and reconcile.
  • Nothing a seller pays for changes the public endpoint data. Alerts and readiness pages read the same observations for everyone.
