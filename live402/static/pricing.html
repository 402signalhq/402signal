Pricing

Pay per check. Pay nothing for a check that finds nothing.

One price for the permissionless path, paid with the same wallet your agent already holds. Platform plans are priced on the receipts you issue and the records you keep, because that is what you come back for.

Per check

$0.003 USDC

Per qualifying hosted check.
No subscription. No account.

A qualifying check is one that finds a live, payable offer that meets your rules and returns the selected terms, with a signed receipt when you ask for require_route_binding. Completed no-match checks are free: live: false with a typed reason costs nothing and settles nothing.

A hosted session open costs $0.005 USDC and reuses one observation for 20 hops or 10 minutes. Hops inside that window do not charge another 402Signal fee and do not call a facilitator. Supported session continuation does not buy another observation for every call.

Catalog search, listed-endpoint validation, the endpoint pages, the offline buyer checks and receipt verification carry no 402Signal fee.

Paid on Base, Solana or Algorand MainNet over x402, from the wallet that will pay the seller. Billing states and uncertain outcomes · Supported profiles and release status

What the fee is and is not

One fee, clearly bounded.

  • Seller charges, deposits, network fees and rent are separate from the checking fee and are paid by your wallet to the seller directly.
  • A later buyer refusal does not reverse an already-settled check. A qualifying offer is not a guarantee of delivery or output quality.
  • If a settlement ends in an unknown state, never reuse that authorization; use the read-only recovery contract and reconcile.
  • Nothing a seller pays for changes the public endpoint data. Alerts and readiness pages read the same observations for everyone.
