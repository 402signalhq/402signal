Before payment

A $2,000 job becomes a $20,000 bill.

Your agent plans 100,000 data lookups at two cents each. The seller changes the offer to twenty cents. A buyer that signs the new price without checking its limits could turn a $2,000 task into $20,000, before fees.

Keep the limit in trusted buyer code. With the supported guard in the signing path, changed terms stop the callback rather than quietly becoming a new deal.

Add the pre-sign check
After an unexpected purchase

The agent says it followed the rules. Did it?

The bill is higher than expected. The agent's summary says the limit never changed. A retained 402Signal record lets you inspect the rules actually submitted and the offer that was checked, even if a local report was edited later.

Compare that record with your own policy and payment records. It is evidence of the check, not a recording of every agent action.
