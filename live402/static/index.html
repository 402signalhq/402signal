Before money moves

One check before choosing. Another before signing.

x402 lets software pay for APIs. 402Signal helps your buyer check the offer it is about to accept.

  1. 1

    Find an API

    Describe a capability or provide an endpoint. Add your allowed network, price and other limits.

  2. 2

    Check it against your rules

    402Signal checks the endpoint's current response and payment terms. You get a qualifying route or a specific reason nothing matched.

  3. 3

    Verify the offer before signing

    Integrate the optional buyer guard to compare a fresh seller offer and the exact request with signed evidence. Changed terms or expired evidence stop the payment handoff. Your wallet still validates and signs the actual transaction.

A simple example

Your agent finds a weather API for 2 cents.

The check finds an offer within your budget. Before you sign, the seller asks for 3 cents or changes the recipient. The integrated guard rejects that changed offer, giving your app a reason to stop.

A successful check is evidence about the observed offer. It does not guarantee delivery or the quality of the weather data.
