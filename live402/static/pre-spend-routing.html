A note from the builder

Before an agent pays, someone should check the quote.

September 5, 2026 · Ross

Finding an API is a useful first step. Before a buyer spends, it also needs to know what the endpoint is offering now.

A listing is the start of a decision

Is the endpoint responding? Does it return a payment offer on a network the buyer supports? Is the price within budget, and can the buyer form the request? These are concrete questions to answer before signing.

That is the role of 402Signal. Send a capability or URL and your constraints. It checks candidate offers during that request, applies those constraints and returns a qualifying route or a reason to stop.

Keep the last decision with the buyer

An offer can change between discovery and purchase. With bound evidence and the optional local guard integrated, the buyer compares the seller's current offer and exact request with the signed observation. A changed recipient, price or request, or expired evidence, stops the payment handoff.

The buyer still validates the actual transaction, controls its budget and signs in its own wallet. The check does not guarantee that the answer will be useful, operate escrow or decide whether a refund is owed.

Pay for the observation; keep its record

A qualifying observation costs $0.003 USDC. A normal typed miss costs $0. The seller's charge and any network fees are separate. The routing fee pays for the check even if the buyer then decides not to purchase.

A payment receipt records money moving. Routing evidence records what was observed and why the offer passed the buyer's rules. Commitments in the public log make changes to a saved private record detectable.

The immediate receipt uses an Ed25519 log signature. Falcon-1024 authorizes the checkpoint transaction anchored on Algorand MainNet. Confirmation of that later checkpoint is separate from the route response and from the seller's payment.

Keep your verification record privately. Private replay records can retain it, but they are not a recovery service. The public commitment cannot reconstruct your private record.

If you are building an agent buyer, an SDK or a payment policy, start with a request your app already makes. The useful test is whether a current observation and a reason to stop improve that purchase decision.
