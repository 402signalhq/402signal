Privacy

What this service stores.

402Signal is a pre-flight check for agent payments. Your agent keeps the wallet. We do not take custody of funds.

Stored to run the check

The requested endpoint or catalog target, route outcome, payment proof needed to bill a successful check, and replay fields that stop the same authorization from being used twice. Decision history may be written to an append-only log and anchored on Algorand MainNet. That log is public evidence, not a customer dossier.

Do not send

Seed phrases, wallet private keys, payment headers, or other people’s customer records. Do not email those.

Who operates this service

402Signal is a trading name. The service is operated by an individual based in Virginia, United States, and this policy is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. The contact for anything on this page is ross@402signal.com. Do not send secrets to that address.

Use and retention

We do not sell personal data. We do not use the check as an advertising profile. Logs and databases live on the systems that serve 402signal.com, with rolling off-host backups of the same databases. Retention periods, as implemented:
