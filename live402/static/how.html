PQ Trust and human oversight

Investigate the decision, not just the agent's explanation.

An unexpected bill raises three questions: what rules did the buyer submit, what offer did it check, and what did it actually pay? 402Signal's retained record helps answer the first two. Your wallet and execution records supply the third.

For a bound check, keep the original request and full response, including the private receipt and reveal, in storage controlled by your operator. Verify the saved record against a log key obtained through trusted configuration. Compare the submitted rules with your separately retained approved policy.

Immediate signed evidence
The receipt and inclusion proof support verification of the recorded observation. Editing its covered request or terms breaks verification. The immediate checkpoint uses Ed25519.
Later MainNet anchor
Cumulative checkpoints are anchored on Algorand MainNet using Falcon authorization. For covered records, comparison with the anchored checkpoint helps expose later history changes. Pending is not confirmed.
Long-term retention
Keep the private evidence, trusted key history, verification software and relevant checkpoint/chain evidence. Anchoring is an additional integrity control, not a promise that every component or record lasts forever.

What the log cannot tell you

It cannot recover a deleted private record, observe purchases that bypassed 402Signal, prove the operator approved the submitted rules, or explain the agent's internal reasoning. It is not an automatic agent-monitoring dashboard. A stored observation does not prove a seller payment or successful delivery.

The guard's historical verifier checks its documented receipt format. It does not itself verify the later Falcon anchor. Post-quantum anchoring protects the checkpoint authorization; it does not turn Ed25519 receipts, buyer signatures or seller payments into post-quantum transactions.

What reaches the service

402Signal receives the route request and constraints, inspects supported seller responses, and handles the checking-fee authorization. Supported request profiles may include exact body context. Never include private wallet keys or credentials in URLs.

The public log publishes commitments rather than the full private decision. Short-term response recovery is not a backup service. Keep your evidence outside the agent's writable workspace where practical, and apply your own access and retention policy.

Documented research, not 402Signal customer claims

Why an agent's word is not enough.

In Anthropic's 2025 Project Vend experiment, its shopkeeping agent gave customers a made-up payment account and was talked into discounts that hurt the business. The researchers reported those mistakes themselves. Read the original report.

In a separate study, Anthropic tested models in fictional corporate environments and observed harmful actions despite benign business goals. Those were controlled simulations, not reported customer incidents. Read the methods and limits.

Neither study used 402Signal. We do not claim it would have prevented those events. The narrower lesson for payment software is to keep authority in trusted code and retain evidence you can inspect without relying only on an agent's summary.
