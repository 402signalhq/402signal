How it works

What happens during a route check

Start with a capability or a specific URL. Add the limits your agent must follow. 402Signal checks the offer; your buyer makes the purchase decision.

From a request to a decision

1. Verify authorization
Your buyer authorizes the advertised $0.003 USDC routing fee on a supported rail. 402Signal verifies the authorization and reserves replay protection before starting route work.
2. Find candidates
402Signal searches supported x402 sources, or checks the exact URL you supplied. The free catalog helps you explore first; it is not a fresh endpoint check.
3. Check endpoints
A bounded request observes the endpoint's response and payment offer. Supported request profiles restrict what can be sent. A buyer-designated search body is not broadcast through discovery.
4. Apply your rules
Current terms are compared with your network, price, response-time and invocation requirements. Prior observations can inform selection, but they do not promise the next result.
5. Settle only a winner
A valid live eligible route settles the routing authorization. A normal typed miss is not settled and its authorization cannot be reused.
6. Caller executes
Your buyer decides whether to proceed. With the optional guard integrated, it verifies signed evidence against the fresh seller challenge before its own wallet validates and signs the seller payment.

Know what passed the check

A qualifying route means an endpoint returned an eligible payment offer during that observation. It is not a guarantee about the seller's paid output.

Bound evidence covers the supported request and observed terms. It can help your app reject a changed price, recipient or request before signing. It does not establish whether an answer is accurate, decide a dispute, or reverse a payment.

Your buyer keeps its wallet and signing authority. 402Signal does not hold your funds or operate an escrow account.

Review supported requests and guard boundaries

A clear result includes a billing state

$0.003 only when a valid live route is found. Seller payment is separate. The routing fee pays for the observation even if your buyer later declines the seller's offer.

Nothing qualifies
A completed normal miss returns HTTP 200 with live:false, payable:false, selected_payment:null and billing.settlement_state=not_attempted; it is not settled.
The outcome needs attention
HTTP 503 is for operational failures, incomplete evaluation, required transparency failure after settlement, or unknown settlement. Inspect billing.settlement_state; never reuse an authorization marked unknown.
A response is lost
The optional durable client retains one attempt and its private recovery credentials. Recover that attempt before considering another authorization. Recovery does not promise that an uncertain payment failed.

Keep the evidence with your decision

Ask for bound evidence with "require_route_binding": true. Your buyer verifies the signed receipt and current challenge before signing, within the evidence's freshness window. A rejection does not undo a settled routing fee.

For later verification, securely retain the complete paid /route response. Private replay outcomes can retain the reveal; they are not a recovery service. The public log contains commitments rather than your full private record.
