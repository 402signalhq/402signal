What you are adding

A check, a local guard and a record.

Check. Bring a supported endpoint or discover one. Ask 402Signal to evaluate its current offer against your requirements.

Verify. Request signed binding and compare the exact request and relevant offer in your application immediately before its own payment code signs.

Retain. Keep the private verification record and your original request. The public log is not a backup of that record.

A wallet cap alone does not supply an independent observation or its retained evidence. 402Signal does not replace your wallet's transaction validation or make a compromised buyer runtime safe.

What changes with the payment model?

Base batch settlement
Check the per-call price and channel limits. A deposit is separate capital; voucher acceptance is not a confirmed payout.
Solana MPP sessions
Check the recipient, operator and session cap. The cap is not a per-call price. Your buyer still owns opening, vouchers and closing.
Algorand atomic groups
Bind both item hashes and the total for the supported two-item profile. Atomic chain execution does not promise atomic HTTP delivery.

What 402Signal sees

The service receives the route request and constraints, inspects the supported seller offer, and receives the routing-fee authorization. Supported request-binding profiles can include exact request context. Do not send wallet private keys or credentials in a URL.

Your private response includes verification evidence. Short-term response recovery is not long-term storage. Public log leaves contain commitments rather than the full private decision. Data retention and evidence boundaries.

Your wallet remains responsible for payment. An observed offer does not guarantee the seller's output.
