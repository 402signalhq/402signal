How it works

Check the terms. Then choose to pay.

402Signal checks the API offer. Your app reviews the evidence and pays the seller directly.

Illustrative x402 example

A $0.020 weather request.

Your limit is $0.020 on Base. The API’s offer qualifies, so the $0.003 check is paid. Your integrated buyer guard then compares the fresh offer and exact request before seller signing.

Same request and terms

The earlier check remains paid if you stop. A completed initial check with no match is free.

What changes with the payment model?

Base batch settlement
Check the per-call price and channel limits. A deposit is separate capital.
Solana MPP sessions
Check the recipient, operator and session cap. The cap is not a per-call price.
Algorand atomic groups
Bind both item hashes and the total. Two $0.001 items plus the check cost $0.005 before fees.
