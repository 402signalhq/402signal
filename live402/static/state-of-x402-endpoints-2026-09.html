State of x402 endpoints.

Five named external sellers had an observed price change in the period; the largest relative increase shown was 200%. Then the wider picture for 22,124 paid API listings from the buyer's side: which rails they sell on, how many answer when asked, and how long a challenge takes.

Second edition, data cut on 13 September 2026; labels and arithmetic corrected on 14 September without a new data extraction. Generated from 402Signal's shadow catalog (CDP, PayAI and GoPlausible discovery feeds) and its own probe history for the 30 days ending 13 September 2026. Probes are triggered by buyer checks and readiness lookups, not uniform sampling, so rates are observed, not population estimates. Aggregates and public seller hosts only; no buyer or payer identities. Regenerate with scripts/endpoint_report.py in the public repository.

What this says

  1. Five external sellers had a recorded price change. The largest relative increase shown is 200% at api.agentstools.dev ($0.001 to $0.003). The largest absolute increase is $0.020 at api.kadec0.xyz ($0.030 to $0.050), about 67%. These observations do not establish a customer loss, misconduct or savings caused by 402Signal; existing buyer limits may already reject a changed offer.
  2. Of 349 observed URLs, 50 (14.3%) never returned a valid challenge during the sampled checks; 14 more had mixed results. This URL-level measure differs from the 92.9% probe-weighted live rate and is not population uptime.
  3. Latency to a challenge is measured in seconds at p95 on every rail; the fastest hosts answer in under 200 ms. Buyers that time out at one second will miss live sellers.
  4. Use a customer's actual seller dependencies to prioritize support. Network memberships and unique-resource reach answer different questions; neither measures paying customers.

Method

A listing is a resource advertised by a facilitator discovery feed. A probe is an unpaid HTTPS GET (with a narrowly justified empty POST fallback) from 402Signal to the listed URL; live means the seller answered with an x402 402 challenge; payable means the challenge carried a complete supported offer; invocable adds an input schema. Latency is round trip to the challenge from Fly.io in Ashburn. A price change or recipient change is observed when the amount or payTo in a live challenge differs from 402Signal's previous trusted observation of the same URL within the period; the before and after values come from the observation rows on either side of the change clock. A listed change is a discovery feed's claim for a URL changing (the catalog listing, at its claimed time); listed changes are reported separately and never counted as observed changes. This edition counts 6 observed price changes and 0 observed recipient changes; the one recipient change in the first edition was a listed change. Overall probe and rail rates include all 2,396 recorded probes: 98 labeled organic, 47 self_test, 5 sponsored and 2,246 unclassified. Unclassified does not mean independent customer usage. The named host table excludes labeled self_test/sponsored traffic and 402Signal hosts, but includes unclassified probes. The six-change aggregate includes 402Signal's own listing; the five named external seller changes exclude it. Live endpoint pages can use a later window or different traffic filter and need not reproduce this historical edition. Hosts are named because they are public catalog listings; neutrality means publishing the method and treating every host the same, not hiding the data.
